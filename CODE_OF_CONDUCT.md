# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the Ava Labs team at **contact@avalabs.org**. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
